> **"All ELR tokens returned to the Founder—whether from Reserve, AETHER revenue, or any other source—must be locked for at least 12 months via Mudra. No lock period below 12 months is permitted during the early stages."**

### 5.2 Real-Time Conversion

Every USDT injection into liquidity is instantly recorded as ELR at the market price at that moment.

### 5.3 6-Month Accumulation & Reimbursement

Every 6 months, total recorded ELR is summed. **50%** is sent to the Founder.
