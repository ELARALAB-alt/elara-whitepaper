## 📦 About ELARA NET

**ELARA NET** is a decentralized AI service network. Our flagship product, **AETHER**, is a transparent AI trading robot — every signal, every profit, and every loss is published in real time on our Discord server.

The **$ELR** token is the access key to the entire ecosystem. It is not merely a speculative asset, but a functional payment tool for real products, including premium trading signals and future AI services.

### Core Principles

- **Radical Transparency** – All decisions, wins, and losses are publicly logged.
- **Learn from Failure** – AETHER was built from 300+ documented losses.
- **Community First** – Genesis Witness program and open governance.
