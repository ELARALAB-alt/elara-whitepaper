| Parameter | Value |

| **Applies to** | All governance proposals |

This mechanism ensures that even if a single entity acquires 20% of the total ELR supply, its vote in governance proposals remains capped at 5%. This prevents any single party from dominating decisions, while still enabling broad participation from all token holders.

Core strategic components—including AI prompt engineering and the deterministic filter—will remain closed to protect AETHER's competitive advantage and prevent adversarial attacks. The backtester will be released with a 6-month delay to enable independent verification without sacrificing first-mover advantage.

**Open Source Status Summary:**

| Backtester | Delayed 6 Months | MIT (after release) |

**Core Engine Definition:** The Core Engine is the collection of code handling order execution, risk management, database synchronization, and exchange communication. It is AETHER's "central nervous system"—not the decision-making logic (which resides in the Deterministic Filter), but the infrastructure that ensures every decision is executed with precision and security.

> **Trustee Release Clause:** If ELARA NET shows no on-chain activity from its multi-sig wallets for 12 consecutive months, the AETHER code—including previously closed components, **except for the Core Engine**—will be released to a **Board of Trustees** consisting of the Security Council (5 members), the COO, and 2 elected Genesis Witnesses. The Trustees are responsible for ensuring the continued operation of AETHER and maintaining the integrity of the $ELR ecosystem.

> **Trustee Operational Rights:** Upon activation of the Trustee Release Clause, the Board of Trustees will receive full access to AETHER's operational environment (server, API endpoints, documentation) and the source code of all released components. For the Core Engine, which remains closed, the Trustees are granted **limited operational usage rights** to run, maintain, and fix critical bugs, without the right to modify the core logic, distribute it, or create derivative commercial works. This right is non-exclusive and temporary, valid for a maximum of **6 months** after activation. Within this 6-month period, the Trustees **must** submit an acquisition proposal to the $ELR DAO to obtain full ownership, or return operational rights to the Founder if the Founder becomes active again.

> **Trustee Acquisition Clause:** If the Trustee Release Clause is activated, the Board of Trustees—on behalf of the community—may submit an acquisition proposal for AETHER through the $ELR DAO governance mechanism. The acquisition value in this scenario is set at **50% of the maximum acquisition value limit** applicable as stipulated in Section 6.4, i.e., **1.5x independently audited annual revenue**. The payment is made to the Founder as compensation. Upon completion of the acquisition, the entire AETHER code—including the Core Engine—becomes the property of the $ELR DAO under the MIT License, and the Founder's royalty rights over AETHER are extinguished in accordance with Section 6.4. If no acquisition agreement is reached within 6 months of the Trustee Release activation, the operational rights of the Trustees terminate and all rights revert to the Founder (if the Founder becomes active again) or remain in Trustee Release status without acquisition.

> **Full DAO Acquisition Clause:** The community of $ELR token holders, through the governance mechanism, may submit a proposal to acquire all intellectual property rights to AETHER from the Founder, including the Core Engine. If the proposal is approved and the acquisition payment is completed, the entire AETHER code—without exception—becomes the property of the $ELR DAO under the MIT License. The Founder receives full compensation from the acquisition proceeds, and the Founder's royalty rights over AETHER are extinguished in accordance with Section 6.4. This clause applies at any time, regardless of whether the Trustee Release is activated.

With this structure, ELARA NET does not depend on a single person. The COO ensures execution continuity, the Security Council ensures protocol protection and strategic input, and the community holds control through periodic elections and the Founder replacement mechanism (Section 7.4).
